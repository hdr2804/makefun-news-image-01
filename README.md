# The World in One Day — Friday, 13 November 2026

Entry for make.fun **news-image-01**: sum up 13 November 2026's world news
in one image.

The picture is a newspaper front page for the day. Its masthead reads
"THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — Friday, November 13, 2026", and the collage plus
its printed headlines cover the day's main stories:

- **Iran hits U.S. radar — tankers burn in the Strait of Hormuz**: Iran's
  Revolutionary Guards said they destroyed a U.S. early-warning radar in
  Bahrain while tankers were struck by explosions in the Strait of
  Hormuz — the smoking tankers in the strait at the bottom of the picture.
- **Europe takes a seat at Ukraine peace talks**: European nations join
  the next round of Russia–Ukraine negotiations — the long negotiation
  table with Ukrainian, Russian, European Union and American flags.
- **NVIDIA chief Jensen Huang resigns**: the chip giant's chief stepped
  down amid a scandal and a summons to testify before Congress — the
  giant cracked microchip bursting with sparks.
- **Microsoft outage blanks office screens worldwide**: a global outage
  took Outlook, Teams and Copilot down for hours — the dark office
  towers with error faces in their windows.
- **Pope Leo in Peru**: Pope Leo met abuse survivors in Peru before
  record crowds — the pope in white waving over the vast crowd.
- **U.S. seizes $1.2B Binance hack loot**: the Justice Department won
  court approval to seize over $1.2 billion stolen in the Binance hack —
  with markets rattled, the red chart arrow plunging beside the towers.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python standard library only) copies `art.png` byte-for-byte
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output/entry.png`).
